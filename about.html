Scientific & Astronomical Methodology

To ensure 100% global accuracy without relying on closed-source APIs, AIFortuneHere synthesizes offline ephemeris calculations from the following public-domain mathematical models and ancient texts.

  • 1. Astronomical Algorithms (Jean Meeus, 1991): The global standard for calculating Julian Dates, True Lunar Perturbations, and spherical trigonometric geometric approximations.
  • 2. Chitra Paksha (Govt. of India, 1952): Utilization of the official Lahiri Ayanamsa for precise Vedic sidereal alignment.
  • 3. Surya Siddhanta: Archetypal definitions for Jyotish lunar mansions (Nakshatras).
  • 4. Tetrabiblos (Claudius Ptolemy, 2nd C.): Foundations of the 12-sign Tropical framework.
  • 5. The Xia Calendar: Mathematical model for Traditional Chinese BaZi (Four Pillars).
  • 6. Pythagorean Mathematics: Base matrix for Life Path and power numerology.
  • 7. Open-Meteo (Geocoding API): Open-source geographical API to process exact longitude and latitude for any global village, district, or city.
